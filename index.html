How It Works

Three steps to clearer thinking

Content-Verify works quietly in the background — no effort required from the reader.

01
🔍

Detection

The extension scans page content and identifies controversial statements using AI, prioritizing 3–4 to avoid overwhelming a reader.

02
🎨

Highlighting

Controversial text is marked inline — from soft amber for mild controversy to vivid red for highly contested statements.

03
⚖️

Analysis

Hovering over a highlighted passage reveals supporting and opposing positions — helping readers understand the full landscape of thought.
